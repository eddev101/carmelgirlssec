Boarding Life

A home away from home

Our new students' dormitory, kitchen and dining hall were completed in 2025, giving students comfortable accommodation and essential services in a safe, supervised environment under the care of the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel.

  • New dormitory completed in 2025
  • Kitchen and dining hall serving daily meals
  • Supervision and pastoral care day and night
  • Routines that build discipline and personal responsibility
  • Academic guidance and support from teachers

Spiritual Life

Growing in faith

Guided by the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel, prayer and religious formation help students become spiritually grounded and develop a strong moral compass.

Leadership

Elected student leaders — Head Girl, prefects and class monitors — learn responsibility and serve their peers through the student council (Baraza la Wanafunzi).

Discipline & Conduct

A respectful, orderly community

Clear expectations allow every girl to learn and thrive. Students are expected to:

  • Wear the full school uniform neatly at all times
  • Be punctual for classes, preps and activities
  • Respect teachers, staff, fellow students and property
  • Use English as the main language of communication on campus
  • Leave mobile phones and electronic devices at home (boarders)

The full school rules are included in the joining instructions.
