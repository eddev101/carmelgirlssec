About Our School

A young school with a big mission: educating and empowering girls for a brighter future.

Introduction

Who we are

Carmel Girls' Secondary School is a girls' secondary school in Olasiti Ward, Arusha District, Arusha Region, Tanzania.

The school is owned and managed by the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel. It is a single-sex school dedicated to providing quality education to female students while promoting academic excellence, moral values, discipline and holistic development.

The school is officially registered under Registration Number S.5944, with NECTA Centre Number S.6697.

Establishment

How it began

Carmel Girls' Secondary School was officially registered on 9th November 2022 and began academic activities in January 2023 with its first Form One class of 73 students. At its founding the school had seven teachers and four Sisters of Apostolic Carmel.

The first Head of School was Sister Ann Nthambi Makundi, AC, who played an important role in laying the foundation for the school's academic and administrative development.

From the start the school offered twelve subjects across three areas of study: Sciences, Arts and Business Studies. It began with basic academic facilities and no dormitory, dining hall, kitchen or multipurpose hall. Even so, management, teachers, Sisters and students worked together to build a strong academic foundation and a good learning environment.

Milestones

Our journey so far

9 November 2022

Official registration

The school is registered as Carmel Girls' Secondary School (Reg. No. S.5944).

January 2023

Doors open

The first Form One class of 73 students begins, with seven teachers and four Sisters of Apostolic Carmel, under Sister Ann Nthambi Makundi, AC.

2023

Infrastructure programme begins

Construction of the students' dormitory, kitchen and dining hall starts.

2024

First FTNA: 63 out of 63 in Division One

Every one of the school's first Form Two National Assessment candidates achieves Division One.

2025

Major facilities completed, and another perfect FTNA

The dormitory, kitchen and dining hall are completed. All 106 FTNA candidates achieve Division One.

2026

First CSEE cohort and A-Level building

The first Form Four class sits the Certificate of Secondary Education Examination, and construction of the A-Level building is under way.

2027

First CSEE results expected

The school looks forward to its first Form Four results and to continuing its record of excellence.

Today

Where we are now

Since 2023 the school has grown significantly in enrolment, staffing and infrastructure. It now has 430 students across four classes and 15 teachers, who work together on effective teaching and learning, academic guidance and the holistic development of every student.

Growing from 73 students in 2023 to 430 today shows the school's development and its increasing contribution to girls' education.

Our Vision

To become a centre of academic excellence and holistic education for girls: strengthening academic performance, improving infrastructure and expanding educational opportunities.

Our Mission

To educate and empower girls through quality education, strong moral values, discipline and holistic development, building a brighter future for girls and society at large.

Our Goal for Every Girl

To nurture students who are academically competent, morally upright, spiritually grounded and socially responsible.

Looking ahead: the A-Level building now under construction is an important step towards this vision. It will let the school expand its services and keep providing quality education to future generations of girls.
