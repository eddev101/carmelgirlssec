Our Approach

Learning that builds a strong foundation

We follow the national curriculum for Ordinary Level (Form 1–4). Students sit national examinations set by the National Examinations Council of Tanzania (NECTA): the Form Two National Assessment (FTNA) and the Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) in Form Four.

  • Regular tests, monthly examinations and terminal examinations
  • Supervised morning and evening study sessions
  • Academic guidance from 15 dedicated teachers
  • Progress reports and parent–teacher communication

An A-Level building is under construction. Advanced Level programmes will be announced here once they are ready.
