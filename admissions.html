Admissions

We welcome girls who are eager to learn, ready to grow, and willing to serve.

How to Apply

Four simple steps to join us

Apply

Fill in the online form below. It goes straight to the school's email. You can also collect a form from the school office.

Submit documents

Bring or send the required documents and the application fee.

Interview

Sit the entrance assessment and attend a short interview with a parent or guardian.

Join Carmel

Successful applicants receive an admission letter and joining instructions.

Entry Requirements

Who can apply

Form One

Girls who have completed primary education and pass the school's entrance assessment.

Transfers

Transfers into Forms 2–3 are considered when places are available, subject to an assessment and a transfer letter.

A-Level Coming soon

Our A-Level building is under construction. Form Five admissions will be announced here when ready.

Documents to submit

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Latest school report / results slip
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Recommendation or transfer letter (if applicable)
  • Medical examination form
  • Parent / guardian identification
  • Proof of application fee payment

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Who owns and runs the school?

Carmel Girls' Secondary School is owned and managed by the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel.

Does the school offer A-Level?

Not yet. The school currently offers Ordinary Level (Form 1–4). An A-Level building is under construction, and Form Five admissions will be announced when it is ready.

Does the school have boarding facilities?

Yes. A new dormitory, kitchen and dining hall were completed in 2025.

How has the school performed in national examinations?

All candidates achieved Division One in the Form Two National Assessment (FTNA) in both 2024 (63 candidates) and 2025 (106 candidates). See the results page.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

Yes, fees can be paid termly. Contact the school office to agree on a payment plan.

What should a new student bring?

A full list of items is included in the joining instructions sent with the admission letter.
