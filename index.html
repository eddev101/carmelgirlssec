Welcome to Carmel Girls

Quality education for girls, rooted in faith and discipline

From 73 Form One students in 2023 to 430 students today, our school has grown quickly, and so has our record of excellence.

Carmel Girls' Secondary School is a single-sex school dedicated to providing quality education to girls while promoting academic excellence, moral values, discipline and holistic development. We work hand in hand with parents, guardians and the wider community to help every girl reach her full potential.

  • Ordinary Level education (Form 1–4), with an A-Level building under construction
  • Twelve subjects across Sciences, Arts and Business Studies
  • New dormitory, kitchen and dining hall, completed in 2025
  • Owned and managed by the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel
Read Our History
Why Carmel Girls

Education that shapes the whole person

We nurture girls who are academically competent, morally upright, spiritually grounded and socially responsible.

Academic Excellence

Every one of our 63 FTNA candidates in 2024 and 106 in 2025 achieved Division One: a 100% record in both years.

Faith & Moral Values

Guided by the Sisters of Apostolic Carmel, students grow in faith, integrity, respect and service to others.

Sciences, Arts & Business

A broad curriculum of twelve subjects, from Physics, Chemistry and Biology to History, Commerce and Book-keeping.

Boarding Facilities

A new dormitory, kitchen and dining hall, completed in 2025, give students comfortable accommodation and essential services.

Discipline & Holistic Growth

A structured daily routine builds the discipline, confidence and life skills girls need beyond the classroom.

A Caring Community

Management, teachers, Sisters, students and parents work together to create a supportive learning environment.
